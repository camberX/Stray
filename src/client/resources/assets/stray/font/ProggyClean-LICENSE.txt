ProggyClean.ttf
Copyright (c) 2004, 2005 Tristan Grimmer

This is the ProggyClean font shipped with Dear ImGui
(https://github.com/ocornut/imgui, misc/fonts/ProggyClean.ttf).
Dear ImGui draws it at 13px. Minecraft's TrueType loader measures the em square, and 16px there is that same 13px pixel grid, with one glyph pixel per GUI pixel.
