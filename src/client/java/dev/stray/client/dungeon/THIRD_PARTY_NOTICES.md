# Third-party code in the leap menu

`DungeonParty` and `LeapMenu` are ported from [NoammAddons](https://github.com/Noamm9/NoammAddons)
by Noamm9 (its `DungeonListener` and `LeapMenu`), which is dedicated to the public domain under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The leap logic (reading the heads,
sorting, the slot click and the close afterwards) is kept as in the original; the menu's look and
the teammate POV are Stray's own.

`MaskTimers` is ported from NoammAddons' `MaskTimers` (same author and licence), as it was before
commit `45679592` hid masks off cooldown in every style. Only the Zyryon style is kept.

The M4 (Thorn) features in `m4/` are ported from [BirdAddon](https://github.com/BarefootBird/BirdAddon)
by BarefootBird, an Odin addon, under the BSD 3-Clause licence. Its licence text ships in the jar at
